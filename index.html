PhD Researcher · University of Southern California

Junchao Ma

I design and build legged and humanoid robots, and make them work, from mechanical design and embedded firmware to real-time control and reinforcement learning.

I'm a PhD researcher in the Dynamic Robotics and Control Laboratory, advised by Dr. Quan Nguyen. Over the past four years I've focused on the design and system integration of legged platforms for highly dynamic motion. I now use reinforcement learning to inform both hardware design and humanoid control, with the goal of robots that operate robustly in complex real-world environments.

  • Legged & humanoid robot design
  • System integration
  • Embedded firmware
  • Whole-body control
  • Reinforcement learning
